Methodology & Framework

This project tracks the cumulative cost variables and verified personnel impacts associated with regional actions. The framework utilizes a structured, automated accounting ledger to ensure macroeconomic transparency.

1. Military & Kinetic Operations

Standardized daily theater costs including Naval Carrier Strike Group (CSG) deployments and personnel hazardous duty premiums. Verified expenditures (e.g., interceptor launches, drone attrition) are indexed via our kinetic ledger and automatically calculated into the cumulative total.

2. Personnel Impact Tracking

Figures represent an 'Analytical Impact Estimate.' We utilize official Pentagon DCAS reporting as the primary baseline, which is then dynamically adjusted by an exposure-weighted multiplier (1.25x) to account for documented reporting lags, classification re-categorization, and the exclusion of non-combat operational impacts in official tallies.

3. U.S. Economic Burden & Commodities

The model tracks the financial delta applied to the United States economy (~20.6M barrels consumed daily) based on live Brent Crude market variations against a historical baseline.

4. Veteran Lifecycle Liabilities

Accrues a structural liability model of $90,000 per soldier/year of deployment, mapped against Congressional Budget Office long-term care projections.
